# Demo video — voiceover script

For the Devpost "Video demo link" field. Hard limit **< 3 minutes**, YouTube, public, English.
The silent screen recording is cut to these beats; record voice over it in Premiere.

Pace: ~150 wpm. Total below is ~350 words ≈ **2 min 20 s** of speech, leaving air at the ends.
On-screen captions already carry the same beats, so a missed line is not fatal.

---

## 0:00 — Cold open · the idle screen

> A short-term rental. There's an Echo on the kitchen counter, and a guest who has
> never been in this house before. Every host knows what happens next — the same
> twenty questions, by text, at eleven at night.

## 0:15 — "How do I turn on the hot tub?"

> This house is an MCP server. Alexa Plus talks to it, and to nothing else.
> The answer isn't from a model's memory — it's the host's own instructions,
> with the host's own picture on the screen.

## 0:35 — "The coffee pods are out."

> It checks the cupboard, orders a refill from the host's supplier, tells the
> guest where the spare box is, and posts it to the host's feed. One sentence
> from the guest; four things done.

## 0:55 — "The shower is dripping." → "yes"

> A problem becomes a ticket. The house picks the host's plumber, proposes a
> slot, and books it when the guest says yes. Tomorrow morning the house will
> say: the plumber is coming at three, for the shower you reported yesterday.
> State lives in the house, so the second day knows what the first day did.

## 1:20 — The judges' drawer

> Everything you just saw went through the Model Context Protocol — the
> November twenty-twenty-five spec, over Streamable HTTP, with bearer auth.
> Ten tools, two resources, one prompt. This drawer shows every call each
> answer made. Nothing here is mocked.

## 1:45 — The Harbor Loft · the calendar-aware repair

> One server, three houses. This is a different unit, with its own guide,
> calendar, contractors and cupboards.
>
> Here the guest leaves tomorrow, and the next one arrives the day after.
> So the repair doesn't land tomorrow — it lands three days out, on the first
> day nobody is checking in or out. That's the one rule every host has, and
> no smart speaker knows it.

## 2:15 — Close

> It's live, it's open source, and the whole thing is honest about its limits —
> a fictional property, a simulated supplier, and a visible quota meter on a
> free model. The house explains itself.

---

## Cutting notes

- If the total runs over 2:50, cut the coffee-pods beat first — the hot tub covers
  "host's own instructions" and the shower covers the agentic chain.
- Do **not** cut the Harbor Loft beat. The calendar-aware repair is the only thing in
  the demo that no other entry will have.
- The drawer beat is the proof beat. Hold it long enough to read two tool names.
